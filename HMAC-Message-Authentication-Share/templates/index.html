Security Properties

  • 🔒 Confidentiality: Not Provided. HMAC does not encrypt; the message remains in plaintext and is readable to anyone intercepting the network packet.
  • ✍️ Non-Repudiation: Not Provided. Because sender and receiver share the exact same symmetric key, either party could produce the tag. Asymmetric digital signatures (RSA/ECDSA) are required for non-repudiation.
  • 🛡️ Integrity & Authenticity: Provided. Any modification to the message, timestamp, or nonce breaks the HMAC tag (avalanche effect). A matching tag proves the message was produced by someone possessing the shared secret key.
  • ⏱️ Replay Defense (Freshness): Provided via Timestamp & Nonce Binding. Binding freshness parameters into the authenticated payload and enforcing an atomic server-side nonce cache prevents attackers from replaying captured genuine packets.
